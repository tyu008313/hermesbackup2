# Digital Risk Assessment

Guidance for evaluating suspicious web offers, investment services, and "too good to be true" financial opportunities.

## Assessment Checklist
- **ROI vs. Timeframe:** Offers claiming high, short-term fixed returns are classic red flags for Ponzi or pyramid schemes.
- **Verification:** Search for the service name combined with "scam" or "کلاهبرداری" (Farsi for fraud).
- **Source Identification:** Check if the service relies on new user recruitment or offers guarantees that are mathematically impossible in standard markets.
- **Financial Hygiene:** Never transfer funds to platforms lacking clear regulation, audited history, or transparent business models.

## Workflow for Suspicious Requests
1. **Search Query:** Search for the service name + "fraud"/"scam" in multiple languages (Target language + English).
2. **Review Community Feedback:** Look for reports on Trustpilot or specialized forums.
3. **Safety Warning:** When an offer is highly suspicious, prioritize clear, direct risk warnings to the user.
